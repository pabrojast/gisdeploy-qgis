about=GISDeploy is a hosted web map service (by Aquedra SpA) for consultancies that deliver maps to clients. This plugin uploads vector and raster layers from your QGIS project with their style (SLD), creates a shareable web map that opens in a browser without an account, and lets you push new revisions or roll back to an earlier one while the link and the WMS/WMTS addresses stay the same. You can also load your team's layers back into QGIS as WMS, private ones included. Interface in English and Spanish.
    Requirements: a GISDeploy account (gisdeploy.com). The free plan includes 1 project, 5 layers and 2 GB; paid plans add capacity. Your layers are uploaded to GISDeploy servers. Layers are private by default; a public map link opens without login. The access token is stored in the QGIS encrypted authentication database.
